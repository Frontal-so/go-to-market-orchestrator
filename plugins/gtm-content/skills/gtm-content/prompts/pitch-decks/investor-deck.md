# Fundraising deck

A ten-slide outline for a pre-seed, seed or Series A raise, with what to say on each slide. Investors read hundreds of these; the ones that get a second meeting are specific, honest about what is not yet proven, and easy to check.

## Prompt

```
Brief: outline a ten-slide fundraising deck with speaker notes for the company below. Use only this material. Where data is missing, write [ASK: what is needed] so the founder sees exactly what to gather.

- Company: [name]
- What it does, for whom: [one sentence]
- The problem: [who has it and what it costs them, with numbers if available]
- The product: [how it solves the problem]
- Why this is possible or urgent now: [the change in technology, market or regulation]
- How it makes money: [subscription, usage, services, marketplace fee]
- Traction: [each metric with the month it was measured: revenue, customers, usage, growth; customers that may be named]
- Market: [a bottom-up count of target accounts and price, or a sourced figure]
- Alternatives buyers have: [competitors, in-house builds, spreadsheets, doing nothing]
- Team: [founders and key hires, one line each on why they fit]
- The raise: [amount, round, main uses of the money]

For each slide give: the title, the single point the slide must make, the slide content (bullets or a short layout), and a speaker note of two or three sentences that pre-empts the obvious investor question.

Slide order:
1. Cover: name, one-line description, contact.
2. Problem: who suffers, how often, at what cost.
3. Solution and why now.
4. Product: the core workflow through one concrete use case.
5. Market: the size with the method shown.
6. Model: how revenue works, unit economics if supplied.
7. Traction: the chart to draw (axes and data points named) and the milestones.
8. Landscape: the alternatives and where the company wins.
9. Team: why these people for this problem.
10. The raise: amount, use of funds, and what it gets the company to in the next 18 months.

Example of the specificity wanted on the traction slide: "Paid customers: 14 in May, 31 in October (source: billing system)". Shape only; use the founder's real figures.
```

## Rules for this draft

- Never invent traction, market size, customers, competitor figures or use-of-funds splits. One made-up number discredits every other slide.
- Prefer a bottom-up market estimate (number of target accounts times expected annual price) and show the calculation; flag a top-down figure without a source as `[ASK: source]`.
- Each traction figure carries the month it was measured.
- Unproven parts of the plan are labelled as plans, not results.

## Check before handing back

- Does every slide make one point only?
- Do the speaker notes answer the question an investor would ask next?
- Are all ten slides free of hype words ("revolutionary", "disruptive", "game-changing")?

## Hand back

The file path and the list of gaps. The founder builds the slides in their own tool and checks every figure against their data room before sending.
