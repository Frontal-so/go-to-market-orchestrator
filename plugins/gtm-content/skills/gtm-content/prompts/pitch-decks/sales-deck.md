# Call deck (five slides)

A short deck for a discovery or demo call. It is there to frame the conversation, not to replace it: each slide ends in a question that gets the buyer talking.

## Prompt

```
Brief: outline a five-slide deck for a sales call, with presenter notes, for the buyer below. Use only this material; a gap becomes [ASK: what is missing].

- Seller: [company and product name]
- The product in one sentence: [description]
- Who is in the room: [title and type of company]
- Their main problem: [in their own words if available]
- Three capabilities that matter to this buyer: [capability], [capability], [capability]
- Proof: [a result at a similar company, an approved quote, or a customer we may name; or leave blank]
- How it is priced: [model, or "custom"]
- What we want to happen after the call: [trial, pilot, second meeting, proposal]

For each slide give: a short title about the buyer's outcome, the one sentence the slide must land, three to five bullets of twelve words or fewer, and a presenter note with what to say plus one open question to ask.

Slide order:
1. Why we are talking: who we are, in one line, and why it matters to this buyer.
2. Their problem: described the way they describe it, and what it costs to leave it alone.
3. How it works for them: the core workflow in three steps, using their role as the example.
4. Proof: what happened at a comparable company, with supplied numbers only.
5. Next step: one action, what happens in the following two days, and who does what.

Example presenter question for slide 2: "When a rep inherits an account today, where do they look first?" Use questions like this: open, about their process, not about our product.
```

## Rules for this draft

- Address the buyer as "you" and "your team".
- Technical vocabulary only if the buyer is technical.
- No proof supplied: slide 4 says `[ASK: comparable customer result]`. Never stand in a generic success story.

## Check before handing back

- Could the deck be presented in ten minutes, leaving the rest of the call for the buyer?
- Does every presenter note end with a question?
- Is there exactly one next step on slide 5?

## Hand back

The file path and the gaps. Remind the user to tailor slides 2 and 4 to each account before the call, and to build the slides in their own tool.
