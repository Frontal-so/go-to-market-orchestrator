# Decision deck for leadership

An internal deck that asks a group (leadership, the board, the whole team) to approve a plan. It earns the decision by showing the situation honestly, giving rejected options a fair hearing, and ending on a single clear ask.

## Prompt

```
Brief: outline an internal decision deck, slide by slide, with facilitator notes. Use only the material below. Where a figure is unknown, write [DATA NEEDED: what to collect] so the presenter knows what to bring to the meeting.

- Team or organisation: [name]
- The plan being proposed: [title]
- The room: [leadership / board / whole team]
- Horizon: [quarter / half / year]
- Where things stand: [facts and metrics today]
- The goal: [what success looks like at the end, and how it will be measured]
- Options looked at: [two or three]
- The recommendation: [which option and why]
- What it needs: [people, budget, tools, time]
- What could go wrong: [two or three risks and how each will be reduced]
- The decision wanted today: [what the room is asked to approve]

For each slide give: a title, the one-sentence message, the content (bullets, a table or a simple framework), and a facilitator note covering what to say, what to invite discussion on, and what to write down as decided.

Slide order:
1. Situation: where we are and why it matters now.
2. Goal: the target and the measure.
3. Options: each path with its trade-offs, side by side.
4. Recommendation: the chosen path and the reasoning.
5. Plan: workstreams, owners and dates in one table.
6. Cost: what is needed and what it costs.
7. Risks: likelihood, impact, mitigation.
8. The ask: the decision wanted from this group, as one question.

Example of the level of specificity for slide 1: "Pipeline from outbound: 38 percent of new opportunities last quarter, down from 52 percent". Shape only; the real figures come from the material.
```

## Rules for this draft

- The options not recommended get their strongest honest case. A deck that props up a straw man loses the room.
- The plan table names only owners the user named; otherwise `[ASK: owner]`.
- No consulting filler ("leverage synergies", "move the needle").

## Check before handing back

- Could someone who missed the meeting understand what was decided from slides 4 and 8 alone?
- Does every number in the deck come from the material or carry a `[DATA NEEDED]` marker?
- Is the final ask phrased so the room can answer yes or no?

## Hand back

The file path and the list of `[DATA NEEDED]` and `[ASK]` items to fill before the meeting. The user builds the slides in their own tool.
