# Customer result carousel (LinkedIn)

A customer result retold as a swipeable document post. It earns trust because it shows a specific before and after instead of claiming expertise. Nine slides plus the post text that sits above them.

## Prompt

```
Brief: turn one customer result into a nine-slide LinkedIn carousel and its post text. Use only the material below. A gap goes on the slide as [ASK: what is missing].

- Posting as: [person or company page]
- The customer: [real name only with their written OK, otherwise a description like "a Series B payroll software company"]
- Their sector and size: [details]
- Before: [the concrete problem]
- What was done: [two or three sentences]
- How long: [timeframe]
- Results: [up to three, each with before, after and period]
- Quote: [exact words, role, and confirmation it was approved]
- The reader we want: [who in the feed this is for]

Slide plan:
1. The result in one line, plus a reason to swipe. Example shape: "From 9 days to 2 days per quote. Here is what changed."
2. Who the customer is, in plain words.
3. What was going wrong, and what it cost if the material says.
4. Why they picked this route over the alternatives.
5. What was done, as two or three moves.
6. The first sign it was working.
7. The full results, before and after.
8. The customer in their own words (or [ASK: approved quote]).
9. What this means for the reader, and one specific next step.

For every slide give: a headline of eight words or fewer, three to five short body lines, and one sentence of visual direction.

Post text: three to five short lines that state the result and why the reader should care. No link in the first line.
```

## Rules for this draft

- Slide 1 has to make sense alone in the feed.
- Slides 1 and 7 use identical numbers. Never round a result upward.
- No permission to name the customer: the description is used everywhere, including the post text.
- No result inflation through wording ("transformed", "skyrocketed").

## Check before handing back

- Could each slide be read in about five seconds?
- Does slide 9 ask for one action only?
- Are all numbers traceable to the brief?

## Hand back

The file path, the slide 1 line, and the gaps. The customer approves every number and the quote; the user designs the slides in their own tool, exports a PDF and posts it as a document post themselves.
