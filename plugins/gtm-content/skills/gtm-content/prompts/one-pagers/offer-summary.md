# Offer and price sheet

A page that answers the three questions a buyer has after a good call: what exactly do I get, what does it cost, and what happens once I sign. It goes out as a leave-behind, an attachment to a proposal, or a web page.

## Prompt

```
Brief: write a one-page offer and price sheet. Use only the material below. A gap becomes [ASK: what is missing]. Prices and terms are copied character for character.

- Offer name and company: [names]
- Who it is for: [role and type of company]
- The promise: [the outcome the buyer gets, one sentence]
- Packages (up to three), each with:
  - name: [name]
  - price: [amount and unit, or "talk to us"]
  - best for: [which buyer]
  - includes: [line items and limits]
- Not included: [add-ons, exclusions, or "everything is included"]
- How billing works: [monthly / annual / one-off / usage]
- Any incentive: [annual discount, pilot terms, guarantee, or "none"]
- Package to recommend: [name, or "none"]
- Help after signing: [onboarding, support, check-ins]
- Next step for the buyer: [link, email or action]

Sheet layout:
- Top: offer name, the promise, company name.
- Side-by-side comparison of the packages: what is in each, the limits, the price.
- What you get: a short paragraph on the core deliverables.
- After you sign: three or four bullets on onboarding and support.
- Before you decide: three questions buyers ask at this stage, answered directly.
- Next step: the call to action.

Example of the tone for one package line: "Growth: up to 5 seats, weekly pipeline review, 590 per month billed annually". Specific, countable, no adjectives.
```

## Rules for this draft

- Never invent a guarantee, a free trial, a discount or a deadline to strengthen the page.
- An answer the material cannot support gets `[ASK: our policy on ...]` instead of a plausible policy.
- No vague value words ("robust", "enterprise-grade", "world-class").

## Check before handing back

- Does every price on the page match the brief exactly?
- Is it obvious what is not included?
- Could a buyer compare the packages without asking a question?

## Hand back

The file path and the gaps. Whoever owns pricing checks every number and term before a buyer sees it.
