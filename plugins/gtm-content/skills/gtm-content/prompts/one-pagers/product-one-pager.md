# Product explainer page

A single page that lets a busy buyer understand the product in about two minutes and decide whether to take the next step. Sent after a call, attached to an email, or offered as a download. Under 350 words.

## Prompt

```
Brief: write a one-page product explainer for the buyer described below. Only the material below counts; a gap becomes [ASK: what is missing].

- Product and company: [names]
- Reader: [job title and kind of company]
- Their problem, in their words: [the pain]
- What the product does: [one paragraph]
- Three capabilities and what each one changes for the reader: [capability: outcome], [capability: outcome], [capability: outcome]
- Proof: [a measured result, an approved quote or a citable case study, or leave blank]
- What it connects to: [the integrations that really exist]
- Pricing in one line: [starting price or model, or "on request"]
- Next step: [link, email or phone]

Page layout:
- Name, a tagline of six to ten words, company.
- The problem: two or three sentences in the reader's voice.
- What it does: three to five sentences that start from the reader's outcome, not the feature list.
- Three capability blocks: a bold name, one line on what it does, one line on what it changes.
- Proof: a pull quote or stat, set apart, or [ASK: proof point].
- Connects with: the supplied integrations.
- Pricing: one or two sentences.
- Next step: the action and how to take it.

Talk to the reader as "you" and "your team". Example tagline shape: "Every renewal risk flagged before the quarterly review". If a sentence would not help the reader decide, drop it.
```

## Rules for this draft

- List only the integrations supplied. "Fits your existing stack" does not count as one.
- An outcome under a capability gets a number only if the material supplies that number.

## Check before handing back

- Under 350 words?
- Could the reader say, after one read, what the product does and for whom?
- Is there exactly one next step?

## Hand back

The file path and the gaps. The user lays the page out in their own design tool.
