# Website pricing page copy

The copy for a public pricing page: the plans, the answers to the doubts that stop people from buying, any proof, and a closing call to action. Visitors on this page are close to a decision, so clarity beats persuasion.

## Prompt

```
Brief: write the copy for a pricing page. Use only the material below. A gap becomes [ASK: what is missing]. Prices, limits and terms are copied exactly.

- Product and company: [names]
- Who visits this page: [role, company type, how far along they are]
- Pricing model: [flat / tiered / per seat / usage / contact us / mixed]
- Plans (up to four), each with: name [name], price [amount and unit], best for [who], includes [features and limits]
- In every plan: [shared features]
- Plan to highlight: [name, or "none"]
- Monthly or annual: [annual discount, or "monthly only"]
- Free plan or trial: [terms, or "none"]
- Guarantee: [terms, or "none"]
- Billing, cancellation, refunds: [how they work]
- The three doubts buyers raise most: [doubt], [doubt], [doubt]
- Proof we may display: [logos we have permission for, a review count with where it comes from, approved quotes, or "none yet"]
- Main action: [start trial / book a demo / talk to sales]

Page sections:
- Headline about the outcome the buyer pays for, not the product. Example shape: "Pricing that grows with your pipeline, not your headcount" (only if the model really works that way).
- One or two lines under it that help the visitor choose.
- Plan table: one column per plan, every price and included item, the highlighted plan marked.
- Questions: the three doubts answered first, then two or three practical ones (billing cycle, cancelling, support level). Two to four sentences each, answering the follow-up question too.
- Proof: what goes where (logos, review count, a short quote), or a placeholder when there is none.
- Closing block: a short headline, one line that lowers the risk of acting (only if true), and the button label.
```

## Rules for this draft

- A reassurance line ("no card needed", "cancel any time") appears only if the material says it is true.
- Never invent review counts, ratings, customer logos or quotes.
- Plain answers: "Yes, you can switch plans at any time; the change applies from your next invoice" beats "We're happy to help".

## Check before handing back

- Does every price, limit and term match the brief character for character?
- Could a visitor pick a plan without contacting anyone?
- Is there one main action, repeated at the end?

## Hand back

The file path and the gaps. The person who owns pricing checks every term, and the user hands the copy to whoever builds the site.
