# What we have tried

A short record of each campaign: who it went to, what it said, what came back, and what we will do differently. Skills read it before suggesting a new campaign, so they build on what worked and stop repeating what did not.

Add an entry when a campaign ends (or after its last touch plus two weeks). Two minutes each is enough. Numbers come from your sending tool or CRM report; write them as they are, and write "unknown" rather than estimate.

## Entry template

```
### [CAMPAIGN NAME], [START DATE] to [END DATE]
Who:        [segment, size of the list after exclusions]
Why them:   [the signal or angle, see signal-map.md]
Channels:   [email / LinkedIn / phone], [number of touches]
Message:    [one line on the core idea of the first touch]
Results:    contacted [N], replied [N], positive [N], meetings [N], deals [N or "open"]
Problems:   [bounces, spam complaints, list issues, anything that broke]
Learned:    [one or two sentences you would tell the next person running this]
Next time:  [keep / change / drop, and what exactly]
```

Leave out open rates unless you track them another way: privacy features in mail apps make opens an unreliable count, so replies and meetings are the numbers to compare.

## How skills use this page

- **campaign-builder** checks for a past campaign to the same segment or with the same angle, and says what it learned from it before proposing copy.
- **signal-monitor** and the signal map use the results to adjust which signals get more weight.
- Comparing two campaigns: note the list sizes. With a few dozen contacts, a difference of one or two replies is noise.

## Fictional example

```
### Pagelorn SRE hiring wave, 2026-02-02 to 2026-03-06
Who:        platform and SRE leads at 60 software companies hiring SREs
Why them:   open SRE role in the last 30 days
Channels:   email, 4 touches; LinkedIn view plus connection note for the top 15
Message:    "A second on-call rotation usually means the last incident's lessons live in one person's head"
Results:    contacted 60, replied 7, positive 4, meetings 3, deals open
Problems:   2 hard bounces from an old export
Learned:    the replies came from companies with fewer than 300 people
Next time:  keep the angle, narrow to under 300 people, refresh emails before sending
```
